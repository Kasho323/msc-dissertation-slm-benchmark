## Removed: the research-philosophy source

Saunders, Lewis and Thornhill (2023) was cited in Chapter 3 to support the
post-positivism and pragmatism discussion. Its bibliographic record was verified
(Blackwell's catalogue for ISBN 9781292402734 gives Pearson Education, 23 March 2023,
**Ninth edition**), but no full text was available, so it was **not possible to verify that
the book supports the specific claim** made in that sentence. An open-access replacement
was sought; the candidate identified (Kaushik and Walsh, 2019, *Social Sciences* 8(9), 255,
CC-BY) could not be retrieved from this environment because the publisher blocked
automated access on four separate attempts.

Rather than cite a source whose content had not been checked, the sentence was rewritten
as an explicit statement of the stance taken in this study, and the citation was removed.
The reference list therefore contains 33 entries, all of which were checked against a
primary record.

If the book becomes available before submission, the original citation can be restored
with a specific chapter or page reference.
